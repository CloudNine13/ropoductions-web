- (decision) 2026-09-21 (Epic 6, A-2026-09-21-01): fullscreen save dock defaults collapsed behind its 44px collapse FAB; entering fullscreen collapses, expansion is user-invoked only, idle re-collapses; windowed dock unchanged (dim-only decay, always visible below the canvas). Supersedes the earlier §3 ruling "Fullscreen overlay defaults expanded; collapse is user-invoked only and resets on fullscreen exit" — owner decision Q13. Accepted WCAG 2.4.3 consequence: the collapsed toolbar leaves the tab order, so the affordance is the sole keyboard route and must stay reachable, focus-visible, and announced. Layer 2 chrome-only dim rule preserved verbatim (never whole-element `opacity-*`).
- (decision) 2026-09-21 (Epic 6): admin panel entry point documented in DESIGN §6 and EXPERIENCE (IA `/admin` row, Component/flow, Voice & Tone) — `Shield` icon, neutral header treatment, never `comp-blue` (reserved for `/admin/*`) or `tier-gold`; rendered server-side only for admins resolved from the override table, never `session.role`; `comp` never sees it. Patron-facing `/play` role pill deleted (owner decision Q10b); `/admin` stays 404 for every non-admin (Q9), no separate admin login. Entry labels are locale keys `header.navAdmin` / `game.adminPanel` (six locales).
