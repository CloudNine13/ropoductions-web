- **F5 — criterion or code? F9 gets a drafted story (5.5), FR-20/FR-21 get added to the PRD as-built, and every cut lands in `deferred-work.md` with a trigger that reopens it. No epic reopening as theater; 5.1-5.3 shipped to their ACs.
- **Process diagnosis (Mary, dissenting frame accepted):** the sprint-status lag is a measurement design flaw, not discipline — "the instrument just isn't wired to the merge event." Fix: transitions to done/merged/guarded must require running a check — CI merge-derived story status, and a verify command bound to every retro item before it may flip to `done` (Amelia's caveat honored: dead-export removal's verify is a caller grep, not a test; guarded-write atomicity's honest home is the real-D1 contract suite because the mock resimulates SQL).
